Feature checks

Proof that every safety rule and feature does what it promises. Each check builds a situation (for example a discount above a category's limit, or a customer in quiet hours), runs the real engine and compares the result with what should happen.

Checks run on a throwaway sandbox store with demo customers and a copy of your offer rules: they never change your data, never message anyone on Telegram and never use the AI. A full run takes about 15 seconds.
